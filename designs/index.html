Tiannah's: five holding-page designs

Click any design to open it full-size. Every page is HTML and CSS only, with no JavaScript. The contact forms aren't connected yet, so please don't send messages through them.

  • 1Electric Tide

    Calm and sophisticated. Aurora light drifts over deep navy, with a serif title, a scrawled underline and a safety pin.

  • 2Cover Story

    An editorial magazine cover with a drifting halftone, a huge condensed title, a ransom-note sticker and torn paper.

  • 3Swell

    Gold Coast sunrise. Waves roll from teal to cobalt, with an arch-framed portrait and a neon "Open soon" stamp.

  • 4Zine

    The most punk, on a tidy grid. Stencil type, a duotone portrait, tape strips, photocopy grain and scrolling wordmarks.

  • 5Constellation

    Minimal luxury. A slowly drifting network on porcelain, with a fine serif title and a neon-teal studded border.
